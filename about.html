About SoleStreet

Our Story

SoleStreet is a simple sneaker store created for people who love comfortable and stylish shoes.

We wanted to create a place where finding a good pair of everyday sneakers is simple and easy.

What We Believe

We believe sneakers should look good, feel comfortable and work well with everyday outfits.

Whether you are going to school, work, the gym or meeting friends, we want SoleStreet to have a pair for you.
